# The Weather Shop — Version 1 Phases

## Product promise

Run a tiny weather shop on a floating island. Gather weather motes, deliver the right
weather to miniature gardens, and watch each island become more alive.

## Phase 1 — Playable weather loop (current)

**Question:** Is walking, collecting a weather mote, and releasing it into the matching
garden satisfying enough to repeat?

- Low-poly floating-island greybox.
- Keyboard/gamepad movement through Unity Input System.
- Three weather motes: sunlight, rain, and wind.
- Three matching garden orders.
- Collection, delivery, coin reward, respawn, and simple progression feedback.
- Responsive prototype HUD and readable interaction prompts.
- Local-only Unity MCP relay configuration.

**Keep signal:** a new player understands the loop in 30 seconds and voluntarily completes
another order.

## Phase 2 — Shop and island progression

- Weather shop building and upgrade stations.
- Recipe book with weather combinations.
- Persistent coins, unlocked weather, and island restoration state.
- Multiple garden biomes and a second island.
- Data-driven ScriptableObject definitions for weather, orders, and upgrades.

## Phase 3 — Soothing presentation

- Authored low-poly props and foliage.
- Layered ambient audio, weather loops, and collection cues.
- Better rain, fog, wind, bloom, and day-cycle presentation.
- Camera polish, accessibility options, reduced-motion mode, and localization-ready UI.

## Phase 4 — Replayability and soft monetization

- Daily forecast challenge.
- Rare weather variants and collection book.
- Cosmetic shop for island themes and weather-machine decorations.
- CrazyGames SDK integration at natural breaks only: rewarded bonus and optional ad removal.
- Save migration, analytics events, and platform-safe consent flow.

## Phase 5 — Browser release

- WebGL performance pass: draw calls, memory, load time, and mobile controls.
- CrazyGames build validation and portal integration.
- Tutorial, onboarding, retention tuning, and final QA across aspect ratios.
- Store art, screenshots, description, and release package.
